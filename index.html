🎯 Who Should Buy What?

🟢 Buy Surface Pro (35,000 Rs) IF:

  • You are a STUDENT who takes notes
  • You want TOUCHSCREEN + Pen support
  • You prefer WINDOWS operating system
  • You want a 2-in-1 TABLET + LAPTOP
  • You want BETTER DISPLAY (2K vs HD)
  • You do LIGHT GAMING (LoL, CSGO, GTA V)
  • You want to SAVE 10,000 Rs
  • You need PORTABILITY (very light)
  • You watch 4K VIDEOS / Netflix

🟢 Buy MacBook Air (45,000 Rs) IF:

  • You want macOS (Apple ecosystem)
  • You need LONG BATTERY LIFE (9-11 hours)
  • You want PREMIUM BUILD QUALITY
  • You do basic OFFICE WORK + BROWSING
  • You don't care about touchscreen or pen
  • You want a TRADITIONAL LAPTOP feel
  • You need an SD CARD SLOT
  • You plan to RESELL later (Apple value)
  • You are an iOS/macOS DEVELOPER (Xcode)

⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNINGS

Surface Pro: Windows 10 support ends October 2025 (security updates will stop). Cannot officially upgrade to Windows 11. Keyboard (5-10k Rs) and Pen (5-8k Rs) are EXTRA. No fan → gets hot during gaming.

MacBook Air: Display is NOT Retina (lower quality than many phones). 5th Gen processor is older (2015 technology). Will feel slow in 2-3 years. Thick bezels look outdated.
