RANA SOLAR TRADERS — Your trusted solar partner.

A Reliable Journey Toward a Bright Future — Rana Solar Trader

Rana Solar Trader has become a trusted name in the solar energy sector through reliability, quality products, honest dealings, experience, and excellent customer service.

Our journey began on November 18, 2020, in Kasur, Pakistan, when we established our first branch under the name Rana Solar Trader Kasur. With the trust and continued support of our valued customers, we successfully completed numerous solar projects and built a strong reputation through quality work and professional service.

Continuing our journey, on January 23, 2021, we established our specialized Rana Solar Repairing Lab Kasur, where professional repair and technical services for solar inverters and solar equipment are provided.

On January 18, 2026, after continuous growth and earning the trust of our customers, we proudly established our third branch under the name Rana Solar Trader Mustafa Abad Lalian.

And our journey has not stopped yet. Insha'Allah, our next goal is to establish another branch in Raiwind in 2027, so that we can provide quality and reliable solar solutions to even more customers.

Experienced Solar Installation Team

We have an experienced and skilled solar installation team that understands the technical requirements of solar systems. From system planning and installation to testing and final setup, our team focuses on professional workmanship, safety, and the best possible system performance.

Expert Solar Inverter Repairing Team

Our own repairing lab has experienced solar inverter repair masters and skilled technicians with practical experience in fault diagnosis, troubleshooting, testing, and repairing solar inverters. Rather than focusing only on temporary solutions, we emphasize proper fault diagnosis and professional repair.

Professional Sales & Dealing Team

We also have an experienced sales and dealing team that understands each customer's requirements, system capacity, budget, and usage before recommending the right solar solution. We believe that honest advice is the foundation of a long-term customer relationship.

Reasonable & Fair Prices

One of the major reasons our customers trust us is our reasonable and fair pricing. We continuously monitor market rates and source products from reliable suppliers at reasonable and competitive prices. Our goal is to provide our customers with the best overall value, combining quality products, reasonable prices, professional installation, expert repair, and reliable after-sales support.
