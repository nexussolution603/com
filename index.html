About PAKISTAN FF GUILD

Our history, mission, and achievements

Our History

PAKISTAN FF GUILD was founded in 2021 by a group of passionate Free Fire players. What started as a small squad of 5 friends has now grown into a powerhouse of 50+ active warriors. Within our first year, we broke into the Top 100 PK Leaderboard and have maintained that position for 6 consecutive seasons.

Our Mission

To represent Pakistan on the global Free Fire esports stage. We train the next generation of FF pros through structured coaching, daily practice, and a supportive community.
